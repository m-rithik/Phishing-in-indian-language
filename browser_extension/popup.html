        About, limitations & privacy

        Local by design. Fisher analyzes only content you paste or page text you explicitly select. It does not use network requests or store your input.

        Know the limits. This is a deterministic pattern checker—not a reputation service, live blocklist, or machine-learning model. A quiet result does not mean content is safe.
